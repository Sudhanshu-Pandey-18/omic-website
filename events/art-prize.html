Shared Ground

PaintingSculptureInstallationUAE residents

Shared Ground explores the UAE as a space shaped by exchange between people, cultures, experiences, and perspectives. What do we bring to the place we call home, what do we receive from it, and how do these exchanges shape both the individual and the shared environment?

Submission guidelines

Eligibility

  • Open to artists aged 18 and above.
  • Applicants must be residents of the UAE.
  • Artists of any nationality are welcome to apply.

Eligible media

PaintingSculptureInstallation

Artwork requirements

  • Each artist may submit one artwork only.
  • The artwork must be an original work created by the applicant.
  • It must not have been previously exhibited publicly.
  • It must not have previously won an award in another art competition.
  • Selected artworks must be available for exhibition at OMIC.
  • There is no size limitation.
