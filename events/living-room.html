About the exhibition

An artist’s gaze often travels outward, searching landscapes, cities and ideas for a subject. Living Room asks for the opposite: to look at what is closest. The textures, marks and leftovers of daily life that we see so often we stop noticing them.

Each artist turns this attention to the everyday, finding in what we usually overlook something personal and worth looking at.

Participating artists

Cristina Gabriela

Dubai-based multidisciplinary artist whose practice explores light, silence and the unseen layers of human experience through textured abstraction and symbolic composition. Exhibited widely across the UAE, including ISOAG, Artezaar and Reem Art Gallery.

@joellart_design_project

Daria Avdeeva

UAE-based since 1997, with training across Russia, Bulgaria and Italy. Her work blends classical technique with modern, colourful compositions inspired by still life, portraiture and figurative painting.

@dariadrawsart

Mrs. Helichrysum

Sophia van der Bank is an illustrator, storyteller and self-taught creative based in Dubai, raised on a farm in South Africa’s Drakensberg mountains. Currently working on her first published book.

@mrs.helichrysum

Tanvi Patel

Canadian artist based in the UAE (known as Artscore), specialising in oil and acrylic on canvas. Her work is rooted in observation, inviting viewers to slow down and discover the story held within each piece.

@arts_core17

Seungeun Cho

South Korean artist based in the UAE, specialising in traditional still-life oil painting. She pairs childhood toys with everyday objects to create quiet narratives around memory, home and the passage of time.

@rambit_drawings

Rollie Mukherjee

Indian artist, art critic and poet based in Baroda, trained at Visva-Bharati and Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda. Her multidisciplinary practice explores memory, displacement and identity.

@rolliemukherjee

Nika Sagamonyants

Contemporary artist based in Dubai working with acrylic, charcoal and coloured pencils. Her practice explores the inner world and human emotions, often drawing on family, home and personal memory.

@nikxslex

Tamara Vasilenko

Born in Rostov-on-Don, Russia, in 2001 and now based in Dubai. Her practice investigates identity as fluid and unstable, using hand embroidery and traditional craft techniques alongside a contemporary visual language.

@tvasilenko.art
