About the workshop

A contemporary acrylic workshop led by artist Sami Alkour, exploring portraiture as mood and presence rather than physical likeness alone. Over two hours, work with expressive brushwork and layered acrylic technique to complete one small acrylic portrait on canvas to take home.

No painting experience needed. All materials are included, along with a croissant and coffee for every attendee.

About the artist

Workshop conductor

Sami Alkour

A visual artist and art educator with 12+ years of experience in academic drawing and painting. His teaching combines drawing foundations with expressive, contemporary painting methods.
