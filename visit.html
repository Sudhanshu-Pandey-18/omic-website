About & visit

Where art
finds its home

OMIC is a cultural hub in Al Quoz where cinema, theatre, art, and culture come together — a gallery, a theatre and a cafe in one warehouse, built for slowing down.

  • OMICultureExhibitions, hands-on workshops, the annual OMIC Art Prize and talks with artists working in the UAE.
  • OMICinemaA 50-seat theatre with German sound technology, honest materials and open acoustics.
  • OMICafeGood coffee, thoughtful food, and a space designed specifically for slowing down.

Good to know

When should I arrive?

Please arrive 30 minutes before a screening or talk starts to allow time for seating and parking.

Is there parking?

Yes — paid parking is available at the venue.

Do I need to register?

Places are limited for every screening, talk and workshop. Register on the event page; when an event is full, it closes including the waitlist.

What's included in a workshop?

All materials, a croissant and coffee. Workshops are limited to 15 places, and no experience is needed.

How do I contact OMIC?

Call +971 4 398 3354, message us on WhatsApp (+971 56 185 7647) or email gallery@omic.spot.

Can I eat before the show?

OMICafe serves coffee, pastries and all-day plates, with popcorn and drinks for screenings.

What should I bring?

Keep your ticket ready before you enter the theatre — and a light layer, as the theatre can get cool.
