About the exhibition

Nothing here is particularly unusual. Through colour, material, texture, form, and imagination, the ordinary is not reinvented, but made newly visible.

The opening evening included a panel discussion — Art & Videography: In Conversation — exploring how the moving image is reshaping the way artists document, distort, and reimagine the everyday, and the blurred lines between fine art and film.
