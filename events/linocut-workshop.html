About the workshop

A hands-on relief printmaking workshop led by artist Elizaveta Pugacheva and an assistant. In just two hours, go from a blank sketch to several hand-printed copies of your own design: carving your own linoleum block and pulling your first prints.

No experience needed. All materials are included, along with a croissant and coffee for every attendee.

All materialsA croissantCoffeeNo experience needed

Key messages

  • Learn linocut printing in two hours, from sketch to finished prints.
  • Hands-on: carve your own block and print multiple copies.
  • Choose a prepared design or bring your own.
  • Guidance from the artist and an assistant throughout.
  • Everything included, no experience needed.
  • Limited to 15 places.

What you’ll do

  1. 01Meet the technique through a short intro and live demo.
  2. 02Transfer a sketch onto a linoleum block.
  3. 03Carve the design with safe carving tools.
  4. 04Ink the block and print several copies.

About the artist

Workshop conductor

Elizaveta Pugacheva

An interdisciplinary artist working across painting, graphics and textiles. Her themes include emigration, cultural identity and collective memory, and her practice draws on computer graphics and internet culture, including memes, often to critique online labour while tracing her own changing identity.
